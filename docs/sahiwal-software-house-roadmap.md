- Tier B: 2.5 × Rs 12.56L = **Rs 31.4 lakh MRR**

#### ⚠️ CRITICAL 2026 POLICY CHANGE — Net Metering Khatam

**9 February 2026** ko NEPRA ne **Prosumer Regulations 2026** notify kar diye, jinhone 2015 ke Net Metering Regulations **repeal** kar diye:

| Pehle (Net Metering) | Ab (Net Billing) |
|---|---|
| Export kiye units 1:1 retail rate par adjust hote the | **Import = full retail tariff** (slab + taxes, ~Rs 40+/unit) |
| Grid = free battery | **Export = National Average Energy Purchase Price (~Rs 11/unit)** par bikta hai |
| 7 saal ka agreement | **5 saal** ka agreement |
| — | **Rs 1,000/kW non-refundable concurrence fee** |
| — | Interconnection/meter/grid upgrade ka cost **prosumer** par |

**Iska aapke liye matlab — 3 golden rules:**

1. **System oversize NA karein.** Export par Rs 11/unit milega jabke import par Rs 40+. Size sirf **apni daytime self-consumption** ke hisaab se rakhein.
2. **Software house solar ke liye perfect load hai** — PCs, ACs, servers sab **din mein** chalte hain (10 AM – 6 PM = peak solar). Isliye bina battery ke bhi 70–85% solar use ho jata hai. Battery sirf **load-shedding backup** ke liye chahiye, "bill bachane" ke liye nahi.
3. **Battery size chhota rakhein:** 10kW system par 5 kWh lithium kaafi hai (router, lights, 3–4 PCs, 1 AC = 2–3 ghante backup). 10 kWh bank = extra ~Rs 2.5 lakh, aur uska ROI net-billing regime mein **kamzor** hai.

**Meri recommendation (2026 regime ke liye optimized):**
- **Tier A:** 6 kW hybrid + 5 kWh lithium = **~Rs 11 lakh**
- **Tier B:** 10 kW hybrid + 5–10 kWh lithium = **Rs 15–17 lakh**
- **Payback:** ~3.5–4.5 saal (commercial tariff ~Rs 45/unit + 18% GST ke against)

                                      └── VLAN 40: IOT/CCTV (isolated)

**Operational checklist:**
- [ ] Captive portal (login + payment gate) — MikroTik User Manager ya Wisper/Wifidog

### 5.6 Legal + tax note on co-working income `[VERIFY]`

- Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012 mein **"apartment house management, real estate management and collection of rent services" 5%** par hain; standard rate 16% hai. **Immovable property ki plain letting** PRA ke scope se bahar ho sakti hai.
- Agar aap isey **"coworking service"** (furniture + internet + services ke saath) ke roop mein bechte hain, to 16% ya 5% ka sawal uthta hai.
- **Apne CA se written opinion lein** aur invoice format usi hisaab se banayein. Yeh chhota sa point baad mein bara penalty ban sakta hai.

### Final word

Aapke paas 3 cheezein hain jo 95% logon ke paas nahi hoti:
1. **Apni building** — Rs 5–10 lakh/saal ka fixed cost zero
2. **4 saal ka low-cost runway** — university ke dauran aapka burn almost zero hai, jabke aap skill + clients + savings bana sakte hain
3. **Sahiwal** — kam competition, kam cost, aur ek underserved local B2B market (101 mills + 78 cold storages + 100+ schools)

Aur 1 cheez jo aapke against hai: **time discipline.** 4 saal mein roz 3–4 ghante extra = **~5,000 ghante.** Yeh itna waqt hai ke aap ek world-class specialist ban jayein — ya barbaad kar dein.

**Aaj ka pehla kaam:** GitHub account banayein, freeCodeCamp shuru karein, aur NTN ke liye apply karein. Baaki sab baad mein.
